# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-01-30

This Privacy Policy describes how the entity operating the Insurance Lead Gen AI Platform (“Provider”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Service.

This policy is a production-ready starting point and **must be reviewed and tailored by legal counsel** to reflect your actual data flows, jurisdictions, and vendors.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the Service, including information provided by Customer and its Users, and personal data contained in lead records.

## 2. Roles

- **Customer** is typically the “controller” (or equivalent) for lead and end-customer personal data.
- **Provider** is typically the “processor” (or service provider) processing personal data on Customer’s behalf.

If the parties sign a data processing addendum, that document will govern in the event of conflict.

## 3. Information We Collect

Depending on configuration and usage, we may process:

- **Account information:** name, email, phone, authentication identifiers, role/RBAC assignments.
- **Lead and campaign data:** contact details, campaign metadata, conversation history (SMS/email/voice), disposition/outcomes.
- **Usage and device data:** log data, IP addresses, user agent, performance telemetry.
- **Support and communications:** support requests, feedback, incident-related communications.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, maintain, and secure the Service.
- Process Customer Data as instructed by Customer.
- Monitor reliability, prevent fraud/abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
- Improve product performance and user experience.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

## 5. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Depending on the jurisdiction and context, processing may be based on contract performance, legitimate interests, compliance with legal obligations, and/or consent obtained by Customer.

## 6. How We Share Information

We may share information:

- **With subprocessors** that help us provide the Service (e.g., cloud hosting, email/SMS/voice providers, analytics). We require subprocessors to protect data.
- **For legal reasons** (e.g., to comply with lawful requests or to protect rights, safety, and security).
- **In a business transfer** (e.g., merger, acquisition, or sale of assets), subject to appropriate safeguards.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Service and as required for legal, security, and operational purposes. Retention periods may vary by data type and Customer configuration.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable security measures designed to protect information, described at a high level in [Security](./security.md) and internal controls documentation.

## 9. International Transfers

If personal data is transferred across borders, we will implement appropriate transfer mechanisms where required (e.g., contractual protections).

## 10. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, and portability. Customers may submit requests through Provider support channels, or through Customer directly, depending on the parties’ roles.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or a higher age where required by law).
